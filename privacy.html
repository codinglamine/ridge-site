Privacy Policy

Last updated 8 November 2026

Short version: Ridge runs on your PC. We don't run servers that collect your data, we have no analytics, and we never see your messages, calendar or music.

What stays on your computer

Settings, tasks, notes, and sign-in tokens are stored in a folder in your user profile. Tokens are encrypted with Windows' built-in protection (DPAPI).

What Ridge sends, and to whom

  • Licence check: your licence key and an instance name go to the store you bought from (Lemon Squeezy or Gumroad) to activate and re-check the key about once a week.
  • Google Calendar (if you connect it): requests go directly from your PC to Google.
  • Claude (if you use Claude Code): Ridge reads your local Claude Code sign-in to ask Anthropic for your usage numbers. Nothing else is sent.
  • Weather: your city name goes to Open-Meteo.
  • Lyrics: the song title and artist go to LRCLIB.
  • Sports scores (if turned on): requests to public sports data APIs.
  • Chats: handled by the Beeper Desktop app on your PC; Ridge talks to it locally.

Purchases

When you buy, the store (merchant of record) collects your name, email and payment details under its own privacy policy. We receive your name, email and order details so we can support you.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling Ridge removes the app; delete the .dynamic-notch folder in your user profile to remove all local data. To have your order details removed, reply to your receipt.

Questions: reply to your purchase receipt, or use Feedback on the home page.
