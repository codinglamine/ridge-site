Getting started

  1. Run the installer. No admin rights needed. Ridge appears at the top of your screen right away.
  2. Hover the notch. La Pile drops out with whatever is live. Click the notch for the full panel.
  3. Press Ctrl + Alt + S. Spotlight opens. Try timer 10, an app name, or a question for Claude.
  4. Open Settings (the gear) to pick a layout, a look and a font, and to connect calendar, email and Beeper.
  5. Paste your licence key in Settings › Ridge when the trial ends. It's in your receipt email.

How do I connect my messages?

Install Beeper Desktop and sign in to your chats there. In Beeper, open Settings › Integrations › Approved connections and press +. Copy the token, then paste it in Ridge › Settings › Connections › Beeper and press Enter. New messages appear in the notch and you can reply from it.

How do I connect Google Calendar?

Settings › Connections › Google account › Connect. Your browser opens Google's sign-in; approve calendar access and come back. Your next event shows in La Pile and on the Glance screen, and Spotlight can add events (event lunch with Sam tomorrow 1pm).

How does the Claude part work? Do I need Claude?

You don't need Claude: the Claude features appear only if you use Claude Code or the Claude app. If Claude Code is installed, Ridge offers to connect to it once. Sessions then appear live: what each is doing, what needs you, and your last three messages. Usage and limit alerts come from your own Claude sign-in on this PC and are never sent anywhere else.

I see Windows pop-ups and Ridge at the same time

Settings › Notifications › Hide Windows pop-ups. Ridge then shows notifications, volume and brightness itself. Apps you mute in Ridge keep their normal Windows pop-ups. Turning the setting off, or uninstalling Ridge, gives Windows its pop-ups back.

Will it slow my PC down?

Ridge sits at around 140 MB of memory while closed and frees its pages when you stop using them. The speech model for dictation loads only while you use it. If your PC feels busy, turn off Liquid Glass in Settings › Material, which re-captures the screen behind the notch.

Does it need my accounts?

No. Ridge works without signing in to anything. Calendar, email and Beeper are optional, and their keys are encrypted with Windows' own protection and never leave your PC.

My laptop has no notch. And what about games?

That's the point: Ridge draws its own island at the top of your screen. Drag it to any edge or shrink it to a thin line. Over fullscreen apps it can go thin or hide (Settings › Behavior), and Ctrl + Alt + M switches any time.

Trial, licence, refunds and a second PC

After 7 days the notch keeps working and the full panel asks for a licence key. One key works on two of your PCs; to move it, remove the key in Settings › Ridge on the old PC first. Refunds are given within 14 days of purchase: reply to your receipt email.

How do I uninstall?

Windows Settings › Apps › Installed apps › Ridge › Uninstall. Windows gets its own pop-ups back automatically. Your settings stay in the .dynamic-notch folder in your user folder until you delete it.
